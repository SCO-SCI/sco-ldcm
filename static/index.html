Methodology

Quadratic limb-darkening coefficients are derived by trilinear interpolation of published tables in Teff, log(g), and [Fe/H]. For Johnson–Cousins, Sloan/SDSS, Strömgren, Kepler, CoRoT, and Spitzer filters, coefficients are from Claret & Bloemen (2011) using ATLAS9 plane-parallel models. For TESS, coefficients are from Claret (2018) using PHOENIX-COND spherical models at solar metallicity. For the CBB filter, coefficients are from Claret, Mullen & Gary (2022) RNAAS, using ATLAS9 plane-parallel models. For the CHEOPS filter, coefficients are from Claret (2021, RNAAS 5, 13), using ATLAS9 plane-parallel models.

Power-2 coefficients are similarly derived for Johnson–Cousins, Sloan/SDSS, Strömgren, Gaia, Kepler, and TESS filters, with coefficients from Claret & Southworth (2022, A&A 664, A128) using ATLAS9 plane-parallel models and from Claret & Southworth (2023, A&A 674, A63) using PHOENIX-COND spherical models at solar metallicity. For the CHEOPS filter, coefficients are from Claret & Southworth (2023) using PHOENIX-COND models (solar metallicity only).

Four-parameter nonlinear coefficients (a1–a4) are derived by the same trilinear interpolation. For Johnson–Cousins, Sloan/SDSS, Strömgren, and Kepler filters, ATLAS9 plane-parallel coefficients are from Claret & Bloemen (2011) and PHOENIX-COND spherical coefficients at solar metallicity are from Claret & Southworth (2023, A&A 674, A63). For the Gaia (GBP, G, GRP), TESS, and CHEOPS filters, coefficients are from Claret & Southworth (2023) using PHOENIX-COND models (solar metallicity only). For the CoRoT and Spitzer IRAC filters, coefficients are from Claret & Bloemen (2011) using ATLAS9 plane-parallel and PHOENIX models.

Microturbulent velocity is fixed at ξ = 2.0 km/s. The LSM fitting method is used throughout. No extrapolation is performed beyond grid boundaries. A REST API is available and is documented here.
